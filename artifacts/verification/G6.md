Phase F6 (Real Office Deliverables) has delivered the complete, verified, air-gapped, privacy-preserving deliverable synthesis architecture across tasks **F6-01 through F6-06**.

5. **Template & Output Safety Subsystem (`OfficeTemplateSafetyService`)**: Fail-closed OOXML package scanner rejecting macro code (`.bin`, `vbaProject`), ActiveX, OLE objects, external targets (`TargetMode="External"`), remote URLs, unsafe paths, and unauthorized overwrites.
