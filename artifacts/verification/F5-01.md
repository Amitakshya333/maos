## Schema Summary

### Bounds Constants
- `KB_MAX_SOURCE_BYTES`: 50 MiB
- `KB_MAX_DOCUMENT_COUNT`: 5,000
- `KB_MAX_PAGES_PER_DOCUMENT`: 2,000
- `KB_MAX_EXTRACTED_TEXT_BYTES`: 10 MiB
- `KB_MAX_CHUNK_SIZE_CHARS`: 2,048
- `KB_MAX_CHUNKS_PER_DOCUMENT`: 10,000
- `KB_MAX_TOTAL_CORPUS_BYTES`: 2 GiB
- `KB_MAX_PATH_LENGTH`: 1,024
- `KB_MAX_ROOTS`: 20
- `KB_CHUNK_OVERLAP_CHARS`: 200

### Type Unions
- `KbDocumentMimeType` — 5 supported types (text/plain, text/csv, text/markdown, application/pdf, OOXML docx)
- `KbDocumentStatus` — pending | indexed | stale | error | quarantined
- `KbQuarantineReason` — 7 reasons
- `KbReindexReason` — 6 reasons
- `KbNoAnswerReason` — 6 reasons
- `KbInjectionCategory` — 7 categories
- `KbRetentionAction` — keep | remove_index | remove_entry

### Key Interfaces
- `KbCorpusPolicy` (schemaVersion 1) — immutable project policy
- `KbCorpusDocumentEntry` (schemaVersion 1) — per-document provenance
- `KbNoAnswerResult` — explicit no-answer with reason
- `KbRetentionDecision` — retention action with `sourcePathPreserved: true`

### Validators
- `validateCorpusPath()` — rejects abs/traversal/network/home/null
- `validateCorpusPolicy()` — full policy object validation
- `validateCorpusDocumentEntry()` — full entry validation
- `validateDocumentLimits()` — per-document bounds
- `validateCorpusCapacity()` — corpus-wide capacity
- `validateProjectIsolation()` — cross-project guard
- `scanForInjection()` — heuristic prompt-injection scanner (7 categories)
- `shouldQuarantine()` — quarantine decision from injection matches
