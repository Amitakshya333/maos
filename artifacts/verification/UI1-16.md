## Executive Summary

Task **UI1-16** delivers the **Evidence & Telemetry Workbench**, an industrial multi-source review and verification interface for inspecting scanned documents, optical character recognition (OCR) facts, vision-language model (VLM) interpretations, and sensor telemetry streams against deterministic engineering thresholds.

The Evidence Workbench directly binds to the sovereign backend services (`ConflictReviewService`, `ApprovalService`, `ArtifactService`, and `AuditService`), enforcing fail-closed safety gating:
1. **Zero Silent Merges**: Conflicting observations and low-confidence metrics (&lt; 0.85) are strictly prohibited from auto-resolving or merging silently into downstream reports.
2. **Complete Provenance Preservation**: All human resolutions permanently retain both original OCR and Vision observations, raw strings, bounding boxes, model/engine identifiers, reviewer identity, timestamp, and mandatory written rationale.
3. **Safety Verdict Governance**: Resolving safety-critical items (vibration, bearing temperature, hydraulic pressure) triggers an authoritative `safety_verdict` approval record in `ApprovalService`.
4. **Read-Only Inspection**: Document previews are strictly read-only; arbitrary drag-and-drop mutations and unsupported Office editing are barred.

## Implemented Architecture & Components

### 1. Document & Telemetry Preview (`src/gui/src/views/EvidenceView.tsx`)
- **Image Preview**: Interactive raster viewer supporting PNG/JPG scanned sheets (e.g. `09_numeric_conflict.png`, `10_unit_conflict.png`, `11_low_conf_ocr.png`, `12_low_conf_vision.png`, `08_agreement.png`) with zoom/pan controls, image dimensions, and interactive Bounding Box overlays (`[x, y, width, height]`).
- **Telemetry Table**: Parses and visualizes industrial CSV telemetry logs (`turbine_vibration_log.csv`), highlighting vibration metrics against ISO limits (Normal &lt; 4.5 mm/s, Warning 4.5–7.1 mm/s in amber, Critical &ge; 7.1 mm/s in red).
- **Text / Document Viewer**: Formatted read-only code/text viewer for inspection reports (`maintenance_report.txt`) and PDF sheets (`01_clean_scan.pdf`).
- **Integrity Badges**: Computes and displays SHA-256 checksums, MIME types, byte sizes, and benchmark fixture classes.

### 2. Extracted Measurements & Confidence Scores
- Renders extracted industrial measurements (`vibration_rms_mm_s`, `bearing_temperature_c`, etc.) from both OCR engines and vision models.
- Visual confidence indicator bars with high-contrast color coding:
  - **High Confidence (&ge; 0.85)**: Green
  - **Medium Warning (0.60 – 0.84)**: Amber
  - **Low Confidence (&lt; 0.60)**: Red with high-visibility alert icon
- Explicit warning banners whenever confidence drops below the 0.85 threshold.

### 3. Engineering Safety Thresholds & Standard Citations
- Evaluates extracted readings against `demo/industrial/safety_thresholds.json` and authoritative industrial baselines:
  - **Vibration Velocity RMS**: Warning 4.5 mm/s, Critical 7.1 mm/s
  - **Bearing Temperature**: Warning 85.0 °C, Critical 95.0 °C
  - **Hydraulic Pressure**: Warning 140 bar, Critical 160 bar
- Official Standard Citations displayed inline:
  - `ISO-10816-3:2009 Clause 4.2`: Mechanical vibration evaluation on non-rotating parts (Zones A/B/C/D).
  - `ISO-13373-1:2002 Section 6`: Condition monitoring and diagnostics of machines.
  - `API 670 5th Ed`: Machinery Protection Systems (Shutdown escalation and voting logic).
- Actionable engineering recommendations for `WARNING` and `FAIL` states.

### 4. OCR / VLM Conflict Review
- Multi-source conflict classification across all 9 domain categories:
  - `AGREE`
  - `CONFLICTING_VALUE`
  - `CONFLICTING_UNIT`
  - `CONFLICTING_LOCATION`
  - `CONFIDENCE_DISAGREEMENT`
  - `AMBIGUOUS_SOURCE`
  - `OCR_ONLY`
  - `VISION_ONLY`
  - `REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW`
- Conservative normalization: letters/digits ambiguity (e.g. `7O` vs `70`, `S5` vs `55`) is preserved and flagged as `AMBIGUOUS_SOURCE`; silent heuristic letter substitution is strictly prohibited.
- Units support: added `'mm/s'`, `'in/s'`, `'m/s'`, `'deg c'`, `'deg f'` to `COMMON_INDUSTRIAL_UNITS`.

### 5. Structured Correction & Provenance Preservation Modal
- Allows authorized reviewers (`lead`, `reviewer`, `operator`, `engineer`, `sec-officer`) to select:
  - `accept_ocr`: Accept raw OCR fact
  - `accept_vision`: Accept raw Vision interpretation
  - `enter_corrected_value`: Enter structured calibrated value and unit
  - `mark_unresolved`: Escalate for physical sensor inspection
  - `reject_both`: Reject both corrupted sources
- **Mandatory Rationale**: Requires non-empty engineering rationale (&ge; 5 characters); submission is disabled without written justification.
- **Permanent Provenance**: Stores both OCR and Vision raw observations, bounding boxes, model identifiers, reviewer ID, timestamp, and audit trail in the Safe Artifact Store.

### 6. Safety Verdict Governance & Approval Integration
- Resolving safety-critical items automatically creates and records a `safety_verdict` approval record in `ApprovalService`.
- Displays Approval ID, scope `safety_verdict`, approved/rejected status, payload hash, and source hashes.

### 7. REST API Endpoints (`src/api/router.ts`)
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/files`: Lists cataloged evidence and telemetry files with SHA-256 and size.
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/file?path=...`: Retrieves file content, SHA-256, base64 payload; rejects directory traversal.
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/thresholds`: Returns ruleset config and ISO/API standard citations.
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/conflicts`: Queries persisted conflict reports.
- `POST /api/v1/evidence/conflicts/compare`: Runs multi-source comparison and persists report (201).
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/conflicts/:id`: Fetches report by ID; rejects path traversal.
- `POST /api/v1/evidence/conflicts/:id/resolve`: Records human resolution and issues `safety_verdict` approval.
- `GET /api/v1/evidence/resolved`: Queries resolved observation history.
